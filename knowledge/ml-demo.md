# Synthetic machine-learning lesson

## What the lesson would show

A small linear model can predict delivery time from distance, number of stops, and traffic level. Gradient descent and Adam can fit its weights, then their result can be compared with a closed-form solution and scikit-learn.

## Everyday analogy

The model learns how much each clue tends to change a delivery-time estimate, much like learning a simple rule from examples. A loss curve shows how prediction errors change while the weights are updated.

## Strengths and limits

This is useful for teaching the mechanics of model fitting on a controlled example. Synthetic data only demonstrates the learning process; it cannot establish real delivery-time accuracy.

## RouteArena status

The machine-learning lesson is planned but not implemented. Any future training examples must be generated from the documented synthetic travel-time assumptions and labelled as synthetic.
